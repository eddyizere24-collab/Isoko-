## 4. Ubunini: icyo Alibaba ikora n’icyo Isoko igomba gukora

Nasuzumye ama-screenshot yawe 6 ya Alibaba. Ibyo nabonye (ni ibyo mpima ku ifoto, si imibare y’abashushanya bayo):

- **Page y’ikicuruzwa:** igice cya mbere cyose ni ifoto/video; ahandi byose ni bito: akarango «Ready to ship», igiciro ≈ 20px 700, «15 pieces» 15px, bar yo hasi ihora ihari ifite 3 ibintu (Chat now, Add to cart outline, Start order yuzuye). **Ntabwo igiciro/umutwe birenga 20px.**
- **Page y’ibyiciro:** inkingi y’ibumoso ≈ 26% y’ubugari (amazina 13px), iburyo gride ya 3 y’uduzingo (≈ 95px) n’amazina 12px. Ibicuruzwa byinshi bigaragara icyarimwe.
- **Urutonde rw’ibicuruzwa:** 2 inkingi ariko buri karita irimo umurongo w’igiciro n’umwe w’inyandiko; ibice byinshi byegeranye. **Ubucucike buva mu bice byinshi (sections), si mu mafoto mato gusa.**

**Isoko ubu (mu kode):** gride y’amakarita ni **inkingi 2 kuri mobile**, ifoto 1:1 ya ≈ 170px; page y’ikicuruzwa ifite ifoto 1:1 yuzuye ubugari (360px), umutwe 24–30px, igiciro 24–30px, buto 48px, kandi «Bisa n’ibi» iri hasi cyane. Ni yo mpamvu wumva ko amafoto n’ibintu ari binini.

## 6. Kwiyandikisha no gufungura iduka (nka Facebook)

- **Intambwe 1 (Konti, amasegonda 30):** «Komeza na Google» (iyo imaze gushyirwaho; bitabaye ibyo ntigaragare) cyangwa email + ijambo ry’ibanga. Izina. Nta kindi.
- **Intambwe 2 (Iduka, amasegonda 30):** izina ry’iduka, **nimero ya WhatsApp** (iyo abaguzi bazakoresha; yemezwa nk’uko byari), akarere. Ihita igaragara kuri rubanda nk’«Ritaragenzurwa».
- **Nyuma (ku bushake, «Uzuza profile» nka Facebook):** ifoto ya profile, ifoto yo hejuru (cover), ibisobanuro, ahantu, imbuga nkoranyambaga (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, urubuga), amasaha y’akazi. Umurongo ugaragaza «Profile yuzuye 60%».
- **KYC/Verified:** buto ya «Saba ikimenyetso cya Verified» muri settings; indangamuntu/TIN zisabwa **aho gusa**, kandi zibikwa mu bubiko bw’ibanga. Ntizisabwa mbere yo gufungura iduka.
- **Profile ishobora gusangizwa:** link nziza (`/s/izina-ry’iduka`), buto «Sangiza» (Web Share API: WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, kopi), QR, n’ibimenyetso by’imbuga nkoranyambaga ziri mu iduka. Icyitonderwa: gusangiza ku mbuga zabo bigaragara neza (ifoto n’umutwe) bisaba **pre-rendering ya Open Graph**; SPA isanzwe ntibishobora. Ni umurimo wa ChatGPT (Edge/Function).
- **Kwinjira:** umutwe w’icyatsi ufite logo y’umweru n’imigongo (nka Alibaba), imirongo mito, ubutumwa bwa inline bw’ikosa.


## 7. WhatsApp: uko igurisha ribera

- Buto «Vugana» na «Gura» zifungura `wa.me/<nimero y’umucuruzi>` ifite ubutumwa bwateguwe: izina ry’igicuruzwa, size/ibara byatoranyijwe, igiciro, ingano, **link y’igicuruzwa**, n’izina ry’iduka. «Vugana» = ikibazo; «Gura» = gushaka kugura.
- Mu igare: igicuruzwa cyose cy’iduka rimwe gihurizwa mu butumwa bumwe (nk’uko bimeze ubu).
- Ubutumwa bwandikwa mu rurimi rw’umucuruzi (rwa mbere Kinyarwanda) kugira ngo abumve, nubwo umuguzi akoresha urundi.
- Isoko ntiyakira amafaranga kandi ntiivuga ngo ikurikirana ubwishyu: inyandiko isanzwe y’«Kwishyura no gutanga bikorwa hagati yawe n’umucuruzi» igume ku page y’ikicuruzwa no mu igare.


## 8. Indimi 6

**Indimi (zisanzwe, zishobora guhinduka):** Kinyarwanda (rw) — isoko y’ibanze; English (en); Français (fr); Kiswahili (sw); Ikirundi (rn); Lingála (ln). Ushobora gusimbuza izindi (urugero Icyarabu/Igishinwa) mbere yo gutangira. Impamvu: abaguzi n’abacuruzi bo mu karere (Burundi, DRC, Tanzania, Uganda) n’abanyamahanga.

- **Imiterere:** `src/i18n/rw.ts` ni yo shingiro; buri rurimi rufite dosiye yarwo (en.ts, fr.ts, sw.ts, rn.ts, ln.ts) **zifite ibyingenzi byose** (TypeScript igenzura ko zifite imfunguzo zimwe: `satisfies Record<keyof typeof rw, string>`). Hook `useT()` na `LangContext`. Ibikorwa byose bya UI bikoresha `t('key')`; nta nyandiko iri mu kode.
- **Guhitamo:** bibikwa muri localStorage (`isoko.lang`), bigatangira bivuye kuri navigator.language, bikagira Kinyarwanda nka fallback, bigashyira `<html lang>`. URL ntihinduka.
- **Switcher (ntiteze akavuyo):** icon ya globe + kode y’inyuguti 2 («RW») ku mfuruka y’iburyo ya header (24px), ifungura akabaho gato (popover/bottom sheet) kagaragaza indimi 6 n’amazina yazo bwite (Kinyarwanda, English, Français, Kiswahili, Ikirundi, Lingála). Iba kandi muri footer. Nta buto nini.
- **Ibigomba kuba biri mu ndimi zose:** navigation, ibyiciro, filters, ifishi, ubutumwa bw’amakosa, imeri y’ubutumwa bwa WhatsApp, amategeko n’ubufasha (static pages), empty states. **Ntibihinduka:** amazina y’ibicuruzwa n’iduka (byanditswe n’umucuruzi), igiciro (RWF ihora ari yo; formatting ya `Intl`).
- **Ibizamini bya CI:** (1) script igenzura ko dosiye zose zifite imfunguzo zimwe, nta gaciro k’ubusa, `{placeholders}` zimwe; (2) Playwright ifata ifoto y’ecran y’indimi zose kuri 360px ngo ihamye ko nta nyandiko yasohotse mu mipaka.
- **Ukuri ku “nta kosa”:** ntiwanyizeza 100% ku Kirundi, Lingála na Kiswahili nta muntu uzi ururimi. Gahunda: ChatGPT/AI Studio zitanga umushinga; **buri rurimi rugenzurwa n’umuntu usanzwe arukoresha**; `docs/i18n/GLOSSARY.md` igaragaza amagambo y’ibanze (Iduka, Igare, Gura…) kandi `docs/i18n/REVIEW.md` ikaranga imfunguzo zitaragenzurwa. Rurimi rutaragenzurwa ruzagaragaza «beta» muri switcher.


## 9. Imigongo n’ikirango

- **Ikibazo:** imigongo ubu ni umurongo wa 4px ufite utunyabutatu duto tw’icyatsi n’umutuku (24×4px). Mu kode ntibishobora kugaragara nk’imigongo; ni yo mpamvu wumva ko byakuweho.
- **Itegeko:** MUST gukora SVG pattern y’imigongo nyayo (diamonds zifatanye, ibikoni/spiral square-wave, imirongo itambitse yo ku nkombe) ifite **tile 48×12px**: ikoreshwa nka (a) umurongo wa 8px munsi ya header kandi ugaragara neza (opacity 100%); (b) 12px hejuru ya footer; (c) imfuruka ya hero (120px, opacity 12–16%); (d) umutwe w’icyatsi w’urupapuro rwo kwinjira/kwiyandikisha (96–120px) ufite imigongo yera/itukura ku nkombe; (e) banner y’iduka rutagira ifoto. Ntishyirwa inyuma y’amakarita cyangwa inyandiko.
- **Wordmark:** «isoko» ya none ni inyuguti zisukuye ariko zisanzwe (typeface). Itegeko: AI Studio MUST gukora **amahitamo 3** yo gushushanya (SVG yashushanyijwe, itari font), ayerekanwe ku mapaji y’igihe gito `/brand-preview`: (A) «o» ya nyuma ifite diamond itukura imbere nk’ikimenyetso; (B) «k» ifite igisenge cy’isoko; (C) imigongo y’umurongo munsi y’izina. Wowe uhitamo imwe; hanyuma /brand-preview isibwa.
- **Ikimenyetso cya app (mark)** kiguma (kare y’icyatsi, diamond y’umweru n’umutuku); kigeragezwe ku 16px (favicon).

## 11. Ibigomba gukorwa na ChatGPT (backend/umutekano)

- **Auto-hide ku birego:** iyo iduka rifite birego bivuye mu bantu **5 batandukanye** mu minsi 7, ibicuruzwa bihishwa by’agateganyo, Admin akabimenyeshwa; si guhagarika burundu. Itegereze rate limit na Turnstile nyayo (#6–#8 mu audit) kugira ngo abantu batarenganya amaduka.
- **Ingingo z’amakosa (strikes):** iburira → guhisha ibicuruzwa → guhagarika iduka; byose byandikwa muri audit log; umucuruzi ashobora kujurira.
- **Isoko ntiryirengera ibicuruzwa byacuruzwa:** mu mategeko y’ikoreshwa (Terms) handikwa ko abacuruzi ari bo bashinzwe ibyo bacuruza n’ubwishyu; ariko **ibi ntibikuraho inshingano zose z’amategeko** (kurinda abaguzi, gukuraho ibirimo ibikorwa bitemewe). Menya umunyamategeko mbere yo gutangaza.
- **Google OAuth:** gushyiraho client ID/secret muri Neon Auth, no kwemeza ko redirect URLs zikora; Facebook ishobora gutegereza (isaba kwemezwa na Meta).
- **Open Graph previews** y’amaduka n’ibicuruzwa (kugira ngo link isangijwe ku WhatsApp/Facebook yerekane ifoto n’umutwe).
- **KYC itari itegeko:** kwemeza ko seriveri itagisaba indangamuntu mu kwiyandikisha; KYC ikomeze kuba ihagaritswe kugeza ibikorwa by’ibanga bitegurwa.
